What actually goes wrong on a first launch

1. Money confusion. A wallet balance number doesn't show network fees, account rent, or what each step costs — so the first create can fail in ways that are hard to read. Our operator walks you through your costs, per your own wallet, before anything is signed.

2. One shot, no witness. When something fails mid-launch you're alone with a red toast message. Our service returns a structured, itemized receipt for every transaction, plus independently verifiable evidence you can re-check with any block explorer.

3. Trust scams. "We launch your token for you" services that ask for your seed phrase are the classic drain. We're the opposite: every signature happens in your wallet, and we never ask for a seed phrase.

4. Growth noise. Before you've even launched, strangers are selling you "volumes", "trending", "makers". None of that is a launch. None of it is what we sell.

Explained plainly: the launch isn't hard because the technology is hard — it's hard because it's your first time, and the circle around you wants to sell you tools, not explain the task.

What we do, step by step

1
We check your wallet and funding before anything touches the chain. Our operator explains rent, fees, and your buy cap per your own wallet, before you sign anything.
2
We prepare the CREATE for you and you sign it locally. You give us your token name, ticker, picture, and description. We build the transaction. You sign it in your own wallet. Your private key never leaves your device — we never ask for a seed phrase, ever.
3
We show you on-chain proof your token exists. Not a dashboard screenshot: the actual transaction signature, the slot it landed on, finality status, and the exact SOL your wallet spent. You confirm this with any block explorer yourself.
4
Only then, a separate decision to buy. If you want an initial buy, it is a fresh transaction with its own approval button, and a fresh price quote at that moment. You've just seen the CREATE evidence. You can decline — nothing happens.
5
You get a structured receipt for every transaction. Each one itemized: network fee, account rent, pump.fun fees, your buy amount. Every lamport is visible and verifiable.

Frequently asked — beginner questions, straight answers

Do you need my seed phrase?No. Never asked for, never used. Every signature happens in your own wallet. If any service asks your seed phrase, that's the scam signature — ours is the exact opposite.
What if the transaction fails or disappears?Then you see it labeled as it is. Statuses are: verified, failed, or unknown — never a silent retry or a "probably fine". Failed-before-signing is not charged (proposed policy). Unknown spend is flagged and reconciled against the chain before any decision about refunds — resolved by the ledger, never a credit balance.
Can I market my token through you?No. We don't sell volume, trending, bots, or any price/holder impact. That's a different industry and we deliberately aren't it. We do the launch execution itself, honestly and audibly.
Why not just use pump.fun directly?You can — it's free and works. What we add is the explanation of each step before it happens, a structured itemized receipt afterwards, and a human to ask — around your own launches, on your own wallet.
Is there any Telegram bot for this?Not for real launches. The old Telegram execution route is excluded from this beta — it was custodial and is not part of this service. An offline demo wizard is in preparation behind a disabled flag; real supervised launches run 1:1 with a human operator through your own wallet only.
Am I guaranteed my token "does well"?Absolutely not. Nobody can promise where a token will trade. We promise only what we can prove: honest execution and a complete receipt of what was spent and what was created. Any marketing service saying otherwise is selling something neither they nor anyone else can deliver.

Stage 3 — Itemized costs and limits

Three different "money" numbers in a launch, all kept separate:

ItemWhat it isTypical

Buy principalWhat your initial buy actually spends on tokensyour chosen amount

Limits — two different kinds, both real:

  • Chain-enforced: the BUY's max_sol_cost is baked into the transaction you sign. The pump program itself refuses to exceed it. This is the only cap the chain enforces per transaction.
  • Application-level (run before broadcast, not chain-enforced): a conservative wallet-debit bound for CREATE, a headroom re-check against your remaining authorized budget, and a durable single-submission lock. These halt before broadcasting — they don't cap anything on-chain.

There is no single on-chain cap that spans CREATE, BUY, and the fee transfer together. That's why each appears separately, at its own approval moment.

Stage 4 — CREATE approval

In ordinary language, this approval says:

"I allow ONE transaction that creates my token () on pump.fun, on Solana mainnet. It spends roughly 0.005–0.006 SOL from my wallet for network fee + account rent. It does not sell anything of mine, approve any other transaction, or give anyone ongoing control. Approving this does not approve any future buy."

What you'd see in a real wallet popup: a single transaction, from your wallet app, with the pump.fun program listed. If your wallet ever shows a seed-phrase request, cancel — that's a scam.
